The studio

Patient geometry.

Founded in 2014, MONOLITH is an architecture practice working between Ouagadougou, Abidjan and Dakar. We believe a building should be quiet, durable, and unmistakably of its place.

Manifesto

We design with raw earth, hard light and long shadows. Not to impress the eye first, but to hold life well — then the beauty follows on its own.

How we work

01

Listen

02

03

We stay on site. Detail by detail, with local artisans and honest materials.

A building is finished when it has lived a full year. We come back and learn.
